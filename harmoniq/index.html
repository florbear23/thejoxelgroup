Harmoniq

Balancing healthcare processes and client engagement. Harmoniq is a HIPAA-compliant application that streamlines appointment reminders, digital intake, and secure document management, working directly inside your existing EHR.

Schedule a Consultation Meet Our Leaders
The Industry Challenge

Industry Challenges

Behavioral health providers face persistent operational barriers.

High No-Show Rates

Without built-in reminders, missed appointments pile up and revenue walks out the door.

Paper & Hybrid Inefficiencies

Paper forms, manual data entry, and spreadsheet workflows scatter your data and slow everyone down.

Low Client Adoption

Traditional portals frustrate clients with logins and passwords, so many never finish.

Streamline Digital Intake and Patient Engagement

Harmoniq is a HIPAA-compliant application that streamlines appointment reminders, digital intake, and secure document management, integrating directly into your organization’s existing Electronic Health Record (EHR) system.

Instead of bolting on another disconnected portal, Harmoniq meets clients on the devices they already use and sends their information straight back into the record. The result is fewer missed appointments, less manual work for your staff, and a smoother experience for the people you serve.

What It Does

Key Features & Capabilities

Automated Reminders

Sends scheduled notifications by text, email, or phone call, straight from your EHR appointment data.

Hassle-Free Authentication

No accounts or passwords. Clients log in securely through a unique link using only their last name and date of birth.

Digital Intake & Uploads

Clients complete assessments and forms and upload images of a driver's license or insurance card from any phone, tablet, or desktop.

Direct EHR Integration

Submitted forms flow automatically into the medical record, with no manual uploads.

Patient Engagement

Built for the Way Clients Actually Engage

Harmoniq replaces clunky portals with a single secure link. Clients open it on the device they already use, verify with their last name and date of birth, and finish in minutes. The result is higher completion, less frustration, and cleaner data flowing into your EHR.

How Harmoniq Works

From a scheduled appointment to a synced record. Hover any step to see what happens.

  1. 01

    Appointment Scheduled

    An appointment is booked in your EHR.

  2. 02

    Automated Reminder Sent

    Harmoniq sends a reminder by text, email, or phone.

  3. 03

    Client Opens Secure Link

    The client taps a unique link and verifies with last name and date of birth.

  4. 04

    Completes Forms

    They fill out assessments and intake forms on any device.

  5. 05

    Uploads Documents

  6. 06

    Information Syncs into the EHR

    Everything flows straight into the medical record automatically.
