Where We Come In

The Problems Healthcare & Human-Services Teams Bring to Joxel

Four recurring challenges we hear from IT and operations leaders — and how Joxel's fractional leadership, EHR expertise, and AI governance address each one.

01

Your EHR runs the organization — but nobody has time to fix it.

The Challenge

Selecting, implementing, or optimizing an EHR usually falls on already-stretched IT and clinical staff, and a misstep affects client care directly.

Joxel’s Solution

Joxel provides expert support across the entire EHR lifecycle: selection, implementation, optimization, and configuration, including hands-on myAvatar and myEvolv expertise.

What’s Different

Joxel's consultants work exclusively in healthcare and human services, not as generalist IT staff learning your systems on the job.

The Outcome

A more defensible vendor decision, a smoother go-live, and an EHR that fits how your teams actually work.

02

AI adoption feels urgent, and risky, with no one owning it.

The Challenge

Most healthcare and human-services organizations have no Chief AI Officer, no governance framework, and no clear place to start with AI.

Joxel’s Solution

Joxel's Fractional CAIO and AI Strategy & Governance services provide executive AI leadership, a prioritized roadmap, and the risk and compliance guardrails to move responsibly.

What’s Different

Joxel builds AI governance around healthcare and human-services realities, including data privacy, regulation, and ethics, not a generic AI playbook.

The Outcome

AI initiatives that move from experimentation to measurable value, without exposing your organization to unmanaged risk.

Take the AI Readiness Assessment
03

Executive technology leadership is expensive to hire, and expensive to skip.

The Challenge

A full-time CIO or CAIO is hard to justify at your size, but going without one leaves technology decisions without a strategic throughline.

Joxel’s Solution

Joxel's Fractional CIO, Fractional CAIO, and Office of the CIO give you the same strategic leadership and governance on a flexible basis.

What’s Different

Joxel's leaders bring healthcare and human-services experience specifically, not a generic executive-for-hire background.

The Outcome

Technology and AI investments that are prioritized and governed, at a fraction of a full-time executive's cost.

04

Revenue is leaking somewhere in the cycle, and no one's sure exactly where.

The Challenge

Authorization, billing, denials, and AR complexity quietly erode margins that are already thin.

Joxel’s Solution

Joxel's Revenue Cycle Management practice takes a 360-degree view from admission through AR to find and close leakage points.

What’s Different

Grounded in the same operational and EHR expertise Joxel applies everywhere else, not a bolt-on generic RCM audit.

The Outcome

A clearer picture of where revenue is being lost, and a practical path to recovering it.
