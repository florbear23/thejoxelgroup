Joxel 365

Small daily actions, lasting global impact. Giving back to our communities and the world, every single day of the year.

Our Commitment

Giving back, every day of the year

The Joxel Group has been a proud member of B1G1: Business for Good since February 2017. Through our Joxel 365 initiative we turn everyday business into everyday good, supporting high-impact projects that reach people and communities around the world.

Giving back is not a once-a-year gesture for us. It is built into how we work, every single day of the year.

Joxel 365

A simple idea with year-round impact

Joxel 365 is our promise to give consistently, not occasionally. Here is what that commitment looks like.

A member since 2017

We joined B1G1: Business for Good in February 2017, embedding giving into the way we run our business.

$1 every single day

Through Joxel 365 we contribute one dollar each and every day, proof that small, consistent actions add up to lasting change.

365 days of impact

Our giving never pauses. It continues every day of the year, all year long, wherever help is needed most.

Why it matters

Small business, meaningful global impact

B1G1 connects the everyday activities of businesses like ours to meaningful giving projects worldwide, from clean water and education to food, healthcare and conservation. Every contribution is tied to a real, tangible act of good.

For The Joxel Group, giving back is not a marketing exercise, it is part of who we are. The same care and consistency we bring to our clients, we bring to the communities and causes we support.

On the Ground

What This Looks Like on the Ground

Clean Water Access

Bore holes and water wells providing life-saving clean water to families in Kenya, Ethiopia, and Malawi.

Education

Classroom construction in Cambodia and Nepal, literacy programs for Dalit girls in India, and early childhood education in Tanzania.

Health

Vitamin supplementation to prevent childhood blindness in Kenya and medical support for nursery school children in Ethiopia.

Economic Empowerment

Business training and microcredit support for women in Kenya and Uganda.

Why It Matters

We believe that doing good business and doing good in the world aren’t separate goals. Every project on this list represents a real family, classroom, or community that had one less barrier standing in their way, clean water to drink, a place to learn, or the tools to build a better future.
