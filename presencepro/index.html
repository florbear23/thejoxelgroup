The Industry Challenge

Industry Challenges

Behavioral health programs run into the same operational barriers again and again.

Outpatient & Day Program Tracking

Many EHRs only track inpatient attendance, leaving outpatient and day programs unsupported.

Leave & Respite Billing Complexity

Tiered, payor-specific reimbursement rates are difficult to configure and easy to get wrong.

Manual Attendance Recording

Paper-based, manual updates slow staff down and invite errors.

Limited EHR Flexibility

Rigid systems can't flexibly handle census tracking and bed management.
